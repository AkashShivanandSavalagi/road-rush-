HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Race left-to-right to the FINISH flag. Fuel drains while you accelerate — grab fuel cans (they fill the tank to FULL). Nitro cans add one charge (max 3). Press JUMP to hop over rocks, traffic and cacti.

Controls — desktop

W / ↑ / Num 8Gas
S / ↓ / Num 2Brake / reverse
A / D or Num 4 / 6Tilt mid-air
SpaceNitro
JJump
EscPause (solo)

Controls — mobile

GAS + BRAKE on the left · NOS + JUMP on the right · tilt ◀▶ in the middle. Landscape required for racing.

Multiplayer

Create Room → share the QR / link / 6-character code → friends join (password optional) → host picks the road, everyone picks a vehicle → START RACE. The host's device is the referee: positions, pickups, the race start and results are validated there. If you drop connection you have 30 seconds to reconnect with your progress preserved.

Scoring

distance×10 + coins×10 + fuel×2 + place bonus (1000/700/400).
