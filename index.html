HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Race to the FINISH flag. Solo: manage fuel — cans refill to FULL; empty tank = 15% power, never instant loss. Multiplayer: no fuel — nitro only, recharged by drafting behind rivals. JUMP hops hazards. Sand slips, mud drags, ice glides, lava burns, green pads boost.

Controls — mobile (landscape)

GAS + BRAKE left · NOS + JUMP right · tilt center · 🎙 hold-to-talk if voice is on.

During the race

No text chat on the race screen — emojis, voice and music only. After you finish, you keep spectating the remaining racers and can switch between them (Tab or ◀ ▶) while you wait.

Rooms

Create → share QR / link / 5-char code → sizes 2–18, duration presets, host vehicle-lock, kick/transfer, bot backfill (clearly labeled 🤖), CPU takeover on disconnect. PLAY races instantly — the name prompt comes after your first race.

FEEDBACK & REPORTS
Privacy: reports contain only what you type plus the diagnostics shown above. No accounts, no identifiers, no tracking. Sending opens your email app; nothing is sent without that step.
